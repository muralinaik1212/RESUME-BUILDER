What we collect

  • Account details: name and email (and your Google profile name/email if you use Google sign-in). Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see them.

  • Anonymous site statistics: total page views, unique visitors per day, and template usage. These are not linked to you.

Why we use it

Only to run your account, save your resumes and understand how the site is used. We do not sell data, show ads, or share data with third parties for marketing.

Who can see your resume

Only you. The site owner can see account names, emails and aggregate statistics, but the database rules prevent reading resume contents.

Where data is stored

On Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore) servers. Your browser stores one small flag per day so a refresh is not counted as a new visitor. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

Your rights

You can edit or delete any resume, and delete your whole account and data from the dashboard (Account and privacy). You may also email us to access, correct or erase your data, as provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
